FAQ

Frequently asked

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope and complexity of the task. Tell me what you need — after a short brief I will give you an exact price and timeline.

How long does it take?

A logo takes from 5 working days, a brand identity from 2 weeks, a video from 1–2 days. We fix the deadline before we start, and I keep it — no rush jobs.

What do you need to start?

A description of the task, your materials and references if you have them. The brief does not have to be perfect — I can help you put it together.

How many revisions are included?

Revisions within the agreed task are free. If the task changes along the way, we discuss extra work separately.

How does payment work?

50% upfront, the rest after you approve the result.
